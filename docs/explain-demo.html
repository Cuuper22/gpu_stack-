Where does the cost of training an AI model come from?

Meta trained Llama 3.1 405B on up to 16,384 H100 GPUs. Its model card lists 30.84 million GPU-hours. Rent that much GPU time at a couple of dollars an hour and you are somewhere around sixty million dollars, before anyone gets paid.

Following one number down

Pick one number: the cost to train a model on one token. A token is a chunk of text, about three quarters of a word. The cost is GPU time, plus electricity, plus the hardware wearing out (amortization, if you like the accountant's word). The math is roughly 6 times the model's size times the number of tokens. The chip depends on how small its transistors are, and that depends on the light used to print them.

What actually moves the bill

The biggest lever isn't which GPU you buy. It's how much of the GPU's speed you actually use, called MFU. A GPU's spec sheet speed is a ceiling. Meta reports using 38 to 43 percent of it for Llama 3. Nobody gets 100.

How the chip is made

The newest chips are printed with EUV light at 13.5 nanometers. The smallest line you can print is roughly a constant times the wavelength divided by the lens's numerical aperture, and lithography is the name for the whole printing process.

How close does it get?

I checked the calculator against 27 published training runs. It usually lands within about 22 percent of the GPU-hours they report. A one-line rule of thumb does exactly as well.

Can a training run survive flaky datacenters?

Big training runs live in one building because the GPUs talk to each other constantly. A clever controller lost to the boring option: let each site train on its own and average their weights every 8 steps. Then the boring option beat perfectly synchronized training too. Averaging weights smooths out the noise you get from a fixed learning rate, and ordinary training with a running average of its weights did even better. A rerun on fresh seeds found the reason.

Keeping the surviving site training through an outage, instead of rolling everyone back to the last checkpoint, did about 3.6 percent less work. Also: the GPU's power reading only refreshed every half second.
