## e003 timing savings read from cost-v1.json

Headline policy timestral_28M|f=0.3|W=24|rho=0.7|q=0.5, lambda 0.5: electricity saving CAISO 8.61% (wholesale mean 35.19 USD/MWh), DE-LU 12.62% (wholesale mean 139.36 USD/MWh at 1.1502 USD per EUR). At lambda 0: CAISO 8.09%, DE-LU 12.12%.
The savings are on wholesale day-ahead prices. A retail price has a wholesale-linked part and fixed parts (delivery, demand charges, taxes); the matched columns apply the saving to the wholesale-linked part only. Demand charges would rise if shifting raises the peak load; that is not modelled.
