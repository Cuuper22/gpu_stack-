## Fetch notes

- nvidia.com product pages (H100, H200, DGX B200, GB200 NVL72) returned URL_NOT_ON_ALLOWLIST from this session's fetch tool and were not retrieved; NVIDIA datasheets hosted by PNY, HPE, Supermicro, and NVIDIA's images.nvidia.com and docs.nvidia.com were retrieved instead.
- eia.gov Electric Power Monthly state tables (5.6.A) could not be read (data is behind spreadsheet links); the Electric Power Annual Table 2.10 (2025 and 2024 by state) was read instead.
- sec.gov filings (CoreWeave, Alphabet, Oracle, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon 10-Ks) are reachable but the fetch tool cuts the text before the financial-statement notes, so useful lives were read from company press releases and third-party renderings of the filings; those rows are marked.
- nectarclimate.com returned a client error and was not used; PG&E and Dominion tariff figures come from OpenEI's U.S. Utility Rate Database pages.
